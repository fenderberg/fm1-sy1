What's new

What changed in each version of SY1, newest first. Installing a new version keeps your user slots, patches and settings. Older firmware packages are on the releases page.

Version 1.0.0 — 2026-11-21

SY1 1.0: one synth, two or four. QUAD plays four sounds at once, made for the Circuit Tracks.

  • QUAD: four parts at once. GLO → MODE now has three modes: Standard (one sound, all MIDI channels), Bitimbral (two parts, L and R) and QUAD (four parts). In QUAD, parts 1–4 listen on channels 1–4 by default: the Circuit Tracks' Synth 1, Synth 2, MIDI 1 and MIDI 2. Each part has its own patch, arp, Hold/Drone, effects, octave and voice limit, and QUAD remembers its own four sounds and settings.
  • Split left and right. With routing on Split, QUAD sends parts 1+2 to the left output and parts 3+4 to the right, effects included, so the two groups arrive on separate mixer channels, such as the Circuit Tracks' two inputs.
  • All of the Circuit's macro knobs. In QUAD the synth tracks' macro knobs 1–8 (CC 80–87) and the MIDI tracks' knobs (CC 1, 2, 5, 11, 71, 74, 12, 13) each move the sound their label names. The manual lists every controller.
  • Circuit MIDI tracks: knobs 5–8 fixed. 0.5.0 had swapped their controllers, so FILTER FREQ, RESONANCE, MODULATION and FX moved the wrong things. They are 71, 74, 12 and 13 again, as measured on a Circuit Tracks with its factory MIDI template.
  • Tighter timing. The audio block is half as long (128 frames, 2.9 ms instead of 5.8 ms), so notes and knobs respond sooner. Parts that are not playing stay on the external MIDI clock's grid, so they come in on the beat when you switch to them or to QUAD. The knobs' glide keeps the same speed.
  • Clearer settings. The GLO pages show which settings belong to the mode, the part and the patch.
  • The Patch Manager shows QUAD when the firmware supports it: four play buttons per slot, Split/Stereo, and four MIDI channel fields.
  • The hardware log (fm1_remote.py log) counts MIDI notes, clock and late messages per input: USB and the TRS jack.

Your user slots, patches and settings are kept by the update.

Version 0.5.0 — 2026-11-18

A new factory bank: 256 presets, a cross-section of all 13 sound categories.

  • New factory presets. F001–F256 are a fresh selection from the 27 community banks: 211 of the 256 were not in the 0.4 bank. They cover all 13 categories of the Patch Manager: 22 each of Bass, Pad, Lead, Keys, Pluck, Arp, Drums and Strings, 21 each of Brass, Organ and FX, 9 Vocal and 8 Other, numbered in that order. A preset is only included when its sound agrees with the category its name gives, and at most two come from one name series (VOICES 001, 002 ...), so no category is filled with variations of one sound.
  • 13 categories on the FM-1, the same as in the Patch Manager: BASS, PAD, LEAD, KEYS, PLUCK, ARP, DRUMS, STRINGS, BRASS, ORGAN, VOCAL, FX and OTHER. ALGORITHM steps through them in that order, then USER and FACTORY; the category on PLAY / SPREAD (KNOB4) follows the same order. Categories the manager writes into user slots (DRUMS to VOCAL) are now kept on the FM-1 instead of being guessed again from the name. Patches without a category are sorted by the manager's own naming rules: "Strings" is STRINGS now, "FM Bell" PLUCK, an organ ORGAN. A browser filter saved on USER or FACTORY stays on it after the update.
  • The Firmware Hub card shows the NORD Play screen enlarged three times pixel for pixel, readable at the card's size, instead of the small GREEN LCD screenshot.
  • GLO → PART, KNOB3 (RANDOM) browses eight variations of the active sound, including any unsaved edits. Each part has its own starting sound. Turn back to BASE to restore it, or press SAVE to keep a variation.
  • MIDI from USB and the TRS jack keeps its timing inside each audio block (fm1-base's midi_stamp), and the voices' age counter no longer wraps around after long sessions.
  • Correct the Circuit Tracks' default MIDI template mapping for knobs 5–8: CC12 is cutoff, CC13 resonance, CC71 modulation and CC74 FX. Previously the modulation knob swept the cutoff and the FX knob changed resonance. The browser demo and manual now use the same physical knob order. This was a mistake, undone in 1.0.0: the Circuit's MIDI tracks send 71, 74, 12, 13 on knobs 5–8.

Version 0.4.2 — 2026-11-17

Two sounds at once, for everyone, and a GLO page with nothing of the patch on it.

  • GLO is now GLOBAL: only settings that are not part of the patch. The patch's voice settings (poly, mono or legato, portamento, bend range, unison, polyphony) and its category moved to PLAY (HOME), subpages 3 to 5; CAT is on PLAY / SPREAD, KNOB4. GLOBAL has four subpages: PART (the part's voice limit, the tempo and the Drone mode, on the same knobs as before), BITIMBRAL, DISPLAY and DIAGNOSTIC.
  • The website, the manual and the README present bitimbral mode as a feature of its own: layer two patches (DOUBLE), send two sounds to the two outputs (SPLIT), or play two tracks of any DAW, groovebox or sequencer on MIDI channels 3 and 4. The Novation Circuit Tracks remains a perfect match, not a requirement. The browser demo is now the bitimbral demo.
  • CI runs every simulator script (basic, performance, nord-preview, ux, display, factory, bitimbral), not only basic; two scripts that had fallen behind the 0.4.0 and 0.4.1 changes are up to date again. A UX review workflow rebuilds the screen gallery in the simulator.

Version 0.4.1 — 2026-11-17

A factory bank you can browse by category: 256 presets, shared out nearly evenly.

  • The factory bank holds 256 presets instead of 500: 35 Bass, 35 Pad, 34 Lead, 34 Keys, 34 Pluck, 34 Arp, 34 FX and 16 Other. The old bank had 100 Bass and 100 Pad against 30 FX.
  • A preset is only included when its name and its sound agree on the category: a pad swells or lingers, a pluck decays quickly without sustain, an arpeggio has the arpeggiator on, a bass starts at once. Names that point two ways ("Kick Bass", "909 Snare w/o Noise"), init and test patches and closed or silent sounds are left out, and at most two of one name series are taken. Other holds only recognisable instruments: drums, brass, sax, flute, horns, chords. The rules are in tools/factory_pick.mjs; every preset renders audible, finite audio.
  • Factory presets are numbered F001–F256. A part that was on F257–F500 starts on the first patch. User slots, settings and the 1,024 user patches are untouched.

Version 0.4.0 — 2026-11-17

Bitimbral: two sounds at once, made for the Novation Circuit Tracks.

  • Two parts, L and R, each with its own patch, arpeggiator, HOLD/Drone and effects. Part R renders on the FM-1's second processor core, so each part has the voices one SY1 had. An FM-1 whose second core does not run plays both on one. Standard mode is SY1 as before and does not use the second core.
  • OCT- and OCT+ together switch between standard and bitimbral. In bitimbral a tap on OCT- or OCT+ puts the panel and the keys on L or R (its light on; PLAY green for L, red for R); holding one moves the octave.
  • Output: SPLIT (L left, R right, each mono, for two inputs of a mixer or the Circuit), STEREO, or DOUBLE (every note plays both patches). GLO's new last subpage has the mode, the output and the parts' MIDI channels (3 and 4 to begin with, the Circuit Tracks' MIDI tracks).
  • The Circuit's eight macro knobs move what their labels say (controllers 1, 2, 5, 11, 71, 74, 12, 13), from the patch as it is in the knob's middle, gliding, with the knob, its parameter and value on the screen.
  • The arpeggiator steps on the MIDI clock, counted from Start, instead of following a measured tempo.
  • Each part keeps its own category, octave, VOICES limit, Drone mode and panel page; the tempo is shared.
  • Settings grow from 16 to 24 bytes; older settings load as they are. An older SY1 installed over this one starts with default settings (the patches stay).
  • tools/sy1_send.py asks again when an answer is lost over USB.
  • The Patch Manager knows the parts. A strip switches Standard/Bitimbral and the output; L and R buttons on every slot make the FM-1 play it on that part, any of the 1,024 (a program change reaches only the first 120); the slot map rings part L green and R red. In the library, L pairs a sound as part L and ▶ then plays others as part R with it in the browser synth; Add pair stages both side by side.
  • SysEx: 08's answer adds the parts' channels and the user slots they play; new 09 makes a part play a user slot. The 03 answer's features byte announces both (bit 1), so an older SY1 never receives them.

Validation: make test, make test-app, the simulator scripts with the new app/tests/bitimbral.txt (mode, parts, channels, outputs, knobs, lights, clock). On one FM-1: the load of two parts on two cores and on one, the mode switch, the part switch and the channel routing. Not yet on the FM-1: DOUBLE, the knobs with a real Circuit, the arpeggiator against a real clock, and a long run of the second core.

The Patch Manager becomes a librarian for the FM-1's 1,024 user slots.

  • Two panes: the library on the left, My FM-1 on the right, with a map of all 1,024 slots coloured by character. Add sounds by drag or button, drag slots to reorder, rename, set a character, remove, close gaps, sort A–Z, group by character or bank.
  • Every edit is staged in the browser with undo. The sync bar lists what will change; Sync writes only the changed slots, each acknowledged, and can be stopped or retried. Removing patches asks first.
  • Sets save whole layouts in the browser, with file export and import, and an automatic backup of the old layout before a sync that moves, changes or removes patches.
  • Preview plays inside the page through a hidden browser synth instead of a new tab; arrow keys and Space audition quickly. The library shows which sounds are already on the FM-1 and in which slot.
  • Firmware: new read-only SysEx command 07 returns a slot's whole record in two parts, so the manager can move and back up any patch, including ones saved on the FM-1. The manager detects an FM-1 without it (0.3.1 and earlier) and then only adds and removes.

Validation so far: library, record and layout checks in host/library_test.mjs; the manager in Chrome against a simulated FM-1 (host/manager_browser_test.mjs). Not yet done: the firmware change and its new check in host/app_test.c have not been compiled or run, and nothing was tested on a physical FM-1.

Version 0.3.0 — 2026-11-16

500 community presets are included in the firmware and browser synth, ready from first boot.

  • Factory 500 is a separate, read-only bank in program flash: F001–F500. From ALL, turn ALGORITHM left once to FACTORY, then browse with PRESETS. The eight original sounds remain.
  • All 1,024 user slots remain available. Existing user records, slot numbers, MIDI program numbers and saved selections are preserved. SAVE makes an editable user copy; erasing user patches leaves the factory bank available.
  • 500 distinct parameter sets from all 27 redistributable website banks, balanced as 100 Bass, 100 Pad, 90 Lead, 80 Keys, 50 Pluck, 40 Arp, 30 FX and 10 Other. Every selection renders audible, finite audio in a two-second four-note host test. Maximum pre-limiter peak: 3.43. This is automated curation and testing, not individual listening tests or a claim of exact original-Synth1 matching.
  • The manager's Factory 500 filter previews the included selection. A downloadable ZIP retains every selected original .sy1 file and complete original source-bank readmes. Full credits and readme text are also embedded in the firmware and readable over SysEx command 0x06.
  • Original author terms remain separate from the MIT code license. The website still offers all 3,174 public presets, original bank downloads and source attribution.
  • NORD, GREEN LED and GREEN LCD remain available, with current screenshots and documentation.

Validation: 202 engine checks; 3,064 app checks including all 500 records, category browsing, user-copy saving, erasing, full user storage, preserved MIDI IDs and read-only credits; 62 reader/ZIP checks; all 500 source files, 27 banks and full readmes verified; 255 simulator checks and 142 screenshots. Baseline audio remains byte-identical. Firmware uses about 359 KB of program flash; RAM data/bss and the audio pool remain unchanged.

Version 0.2.1 — 2026-11-16

Choose the display that suits your FM-1: NORD, GREEN LED, or GREEN LCD.

  • Display choice: GLO → DISPLAY (6/6), KNOB1. NORD keeps the red strip, ivory labels and amber focus. GREEN LED uses green pixel letters and segment numerals on black. GREEN LCD puts dark lettering and graphs on a soft green background, with a lighter focus panel.
  • Same controls and feedback: all 32 subpages retain saved-value marks, units, edit indication, graphs, envelope/LFO/arp motion, and screen-reader text in every style.
  • Remembered preference: saved globally while idle. Older 12-byte settings migrate while retaining the selected patch, octave, tempo, voice limit, category and drone choice. Changing style does not edit the sound or send audio-engine events.
  • Current screenshots: 137 real simulator captures, plus a three-style comparison on the website, updated manual and the full screen gallery.
  • Browser panel: homepage knob styling is scoped to the decorative rack, keeping emulator controls in their original positions and appearance.
  • Patch library: 3,174 presets / 27 original banks remain available with author credits and terms.

Validation: 202 engine checks, 445 app checks, 62 JavaScript/C and ZIP checks, all 3,174 preset files, 234 simulator checks, 32 subpages in three styles, and browser checks for style changes, persisted reloads and screen-reader values. The baseline WAV remains byte-identical. Firmware and WebAssembly build checks pass. The preceding 0.2.1-green candidate was installed on a physical FM-1: 513 slots and the selected patch were retained; LCD selection was confirmed and the device reported no crash. Screen readability and audio load during playing have not been fully assessed. This remains an early release.

Version 0.2.0 — 2026-11-16

SY1 now puts its Synth1 sound library first, with a complete Nord-inspired screen and website redesign.

  • Sound library: 3,174 presets in 27 original community banks, with author credits, complete readmes and original ZIP downloads. Preset terms remain separate from the MIT source-code license.
  • Patch Manager: search, bank and suggested-character filters, persistent favourites, individual or whole-bank selection, and browser-synth previews. Imports .sy1, folders, bank ZIPs and nested ZIP collections locally; tested with the supplied 175-bank / 16,698-patch collection.
  • USB transfer: reads device slots, adds after the last patch by default, checks the complete selection against 1,024 slots, confirms occupied-slot replacement, shows progress, and keeps unsent patches for retry. MIDI responses are matched by command, input port and slot. Stop waits for the current acknowledgement.
  • Firmware UX: consistent patch header, red section strip, sound display, four encoder columns and playing-status footer across all 31 subpages. Saved-value marks, precise edit indication, remembered subpages, units, live envelope/LFO/arp feedback and accessible spoken values.
  • Performance: arp chord hold, drone stack/replace modes, category browsing and stored category overrides.
  • Website and manual: shared navigation across Overview, Play, Patch Manager, Install and Manual; matching firmware, WebAssembly emulator, reader words and installation package.

Validation: 202 engine checks, 220 app checks, 62 JavaScript/C and ZIP checks, integrity checks for all 3,174 hosted presets, 130 simulator UX checks with 57 screen captures, and browser integration checks including the full local collection. Browser USB tests use a simulated MIDI device; the physical FM-1 was checked with the redesigned development firmware while retaining its existing 513 patch slots. The engine's sound has not yet been closely compared with original Synth1. This remains an early release.
