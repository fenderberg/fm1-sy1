02 / BITIMBRAL

Two sounds at once.

Press OCT- and OCT+ together and the FM-1 becomes two synths, L and R. Each has its own patch, arpeggiator, Hold, Drone and effects. Part R runs on the FM-1's second core, so neither part gives up voices.

01 / LAYERL+R

Stack two patches.

In DOUBLE, every note plays both parts: a pad under a bell, a bass doubled by a pluck. One key, a bigger sound.

02 / SPLITL|R

Two sounds, two outputs.

SPLIT sends L to the left output and R to the right, each in mono. Put a bass and a lead on two mixer channels and treat them apart.

03 / MIDI3·4

One synth, two tracks.

L listens on MIDI channel 3, R on 4, and you can change both. Two tracks of a DAW, a groovebox or a sequencer each play their own sound.

04 / HANDS ONOCT±

Switch with one tap.

Tap OCT- or OCT+ to put the panel and the keys on L or R; PLAY lights green or red. Every page, knob and SAVE acts on that part.

Own a Novation Circuit Tracks? Its two MIDI tracks and its eight macro knobs map straight onto the two parts. Anything that sends MIDI works.

VERSION {{VERSION}} / EARLY RELEASE

Ready for a little exploration?

SY1 runs on the M-VAVE FM-1 with about seven voices, and about seven more for the second part in bitimbral mode. It is a lighter reimplementation of Synth1; its sound has not yet been compared closely with the original.

The installer can restore M-VAVE’s stock firmware. Read the manual before installing.
