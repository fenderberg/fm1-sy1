02 / YOUR SOUND COLLECTION

Your patches. Ready to play.

Load Synth1 patches into an FM-1 running SY1, over USB. Your files stay on this computer: the page reads them here and sends them straight to the FM-1.

FM-1 NOT CONNECTED / USB MIDI

01 Connect your FM-1

Connect the FM-1 by USB and switch it on. This needs Chrome, Edge or another browser with Web MIDI.

Ready when you are.

02 Bring your patches

Drop patches here: .sy1 files, folders of them, or .zip banks

    You can prepare your list before connecting. Sending becomes available when your FM-1 is connected.

    Not connected.
